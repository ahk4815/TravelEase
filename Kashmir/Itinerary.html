7 Days in Kashmir

Explore the serene beauty of Kashmir with this 7-day itinerary, covering the best of the region’s natural landscapes and cultural heritage.

Itinerary

Day 0: Arrival in Srinagar

Arrive at Srinagar Airport. Check into your houseboat or hotel. Enjoy a relaxing Shikara ride on Dal Lake in the evening.

Day 1: Srinagar Local Sightseeing

Visit Mughal Gardens – Nishat Bagh, Shalimar Bagh, and Chashme Shahi. Explore Shankaracharya Temple and shop at the local handicrafts market.

Day 2: Srinagar to Gulmarg

Drive to Gulmarg (approx. 2 hours). Enjoy the famous Gondola ride up to Phase 1 or Phase 2 for panoramic snow views. Return to hotel or stay overnight in Gulmarg.

Day 3: Gulmarg to Pahalgam

Drive to Pahalgam (approx. 4–5 hours). On the way, stop at saffron fields and the ruins of Avantipur Temple. Evening at leisure in Pahalgam.

Day 4: Pahalgam Exploration

Visit Aru Valley, Betaab Valley, and Chandanwari (optional pony ride). Enjoy scenic views and river walks in the lush valleys.

Day 5: Pahalgam to Sonamarg via Srinagar

Drive to Sonamarg (approx. 6–7 hours via Srinagar). Enjoy the Thajiwas Glacier trek or pony ride (seasonal). Return and stay in Sonamarg or back in Srinagar (as preferred).

Day 6: Leisure Day in Srinagar

Spend a relaxed day shopping for dry fruits, Pashmina, and Kashmiri handicrafts. Visit Hazratbal Shrine or enjoy another Shikara ride. Ideal day for cultural immersion.

Day 7: Departure from Srinagar

Check out and transfer to Srinagar Airport for your onward journey, carrying memories of paradise on earth.
