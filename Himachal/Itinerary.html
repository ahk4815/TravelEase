7 Days in Himachal

Explore the serene beauty of Himachal with this 7-day itinerary, covering the best of the region’s natural landscapes and cultural heritage.

Itinerary

Day 0: Arrival in Delhi/Chandigarh

Arrive in Delhi or Chandigarh and rest. Prepare for your journey into Himachal Pradesh the next day.

Day 1: Delhi/Chandigarh to Shimla

Drive to Shimla (approx. 7–8 hours). Check into your hotel and enjoy a relaxing evening walk on Mall Road.

Day 2: Shimla Sightseeing

Explore Kufri, Jakhoo Temple, The Ridge, Christ Church, and take a toy train ride if time permits.

Day 3: Shimla to Manali

Drive to Manali via Mandi and Kullu (approx. 8–9 hours). Check into your hotel and relax in the cool mountain air.

Day 4: Manali Local Sightseeing

Visit Hadimba Temple, Manu Temple, Vashisht Hot Springs, and explore Old Manali cafes in the evening.

Day 5: Solang Valley / Rohtang Pass (Seasonal)

Take a day trip to Solang Valley for adventure sports like paragliding and ATV rides. If open, drive further to Rohtang Pass for snow activities.

Day 6: Manali to Kasol

Drive to Kasol (approx. 2.5–3 hours). Explore the Parvati Valley, relax by the river, and enjoy Israeli cuisine. Optional visit to Manikaran Sahib Gurudwara.

Day 7: Departure

Drive back to Delhi/Chandigarh for your onward journey with memories of the mountains.
