7 Days in Ladhak

Explore the serene beauty of Ladhak with this 7-day itinerary, covering the best of the region’s natural landscapes and cultural heritage.

Itinerary

Day 0: Arrival in Leh

Arrive in Leh by flight. Rest for the day to acclimatize to the high altitude. Light walking in the Leh market if feeling well.

Day 1: Leh Local Sightseeing

Visit Shanti Stupa, Leh Palace, and Hall of Fame. Explore local cafes and Tibetan markets in the evening.

Day 2: Leh to Nubra Valley via Khardung La

Drive to Nubra Valley via Khardung La (one of the world's highest motorable roads). Visit Diskit Monastery and enjoy camel rides in Hunder dunes.

Day 3: Nubra to Pangong Lake via Shyok

Travel to Pangong Lake through the scenic Shyok route. Enjoy the breathtaking beauty of the lake. Overnight stay near the lake in camps.

Day 4: Pangong Lake to Leh

Return to Leh via Chang La Pass. On the way, visit Thiksey Monastery and Shey Palace.

Day 5: Day Trip to Magnetic Hill & Sangam

Visit Magnetic Hill, Gurudwara Pathar Sahib, and the Sangam (confluence of Zanskar and Indus rivers). Optional river rafting in Zanskar (seasonal).

Day 6: Leisure Day in Leh

Use this day to relax or explore local monasteries like Spituk or Hemis. Shop for souvenirs in Leh market and prepare for departure.

Day 7: Departure from Leh

Check out from your hotel and transfer to Leh airport for your return flight.
