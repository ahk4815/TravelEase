7 Days in Uttarakhand

Explore the serene beauty of Uttarakhand with this 7-day itinerary, covering the best of the region’s natural landscapes and cultural heritage.

Itinerary

Day 0: Arrival in Delhi

Arrive in Delhi and check into your hotel. Rest and prepare for your journey to Uttarakhand.

Day 1: Delhi to Rishikesh

Travel from Delhi to Rishikesh. Check into your hotel and relax.

Day 2: Rishikesh Exploration

Explore Rishikesh, visit the iconic Laxman Jhula and Ram Jhula, and experience the spiritual ambiance of the city.

Day 3: Rishikesh to Mussoorie

Drive from Rishikesh to Mussoorie. Check into your hotel and enjoy the scenic views.

Day 4: Mussoorie Sightseeing

Explore Mussoorie, visit Kempty Falls, Gun Hill, and enjoy the panoramic views of the Himalayas.

Day 5: Mussoorie to Nainital

Travel from Mussoorie to Nainital. Check into your hotel and unwind.

Day 6: Nainital Exploration

Discover Nainital, visit Naini Lake, Naina Devi Temple, and enjoy a boat ride.

Day 7: Departure from Nainital

Depart from Nainital to Delhi for your onward journey.
